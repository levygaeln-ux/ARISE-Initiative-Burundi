Who We Are

Youth-led, community-rooted

A.R.I.S.E. Initiative was founded in 2025 by Tujenge Scholars Program (Cohort 9). Our team of 14 young leaders brings experience in journalism, storytelling, community organizing, performance arts, and digital innovation.

Every member of our team knows sign language, and one member reads and writes Braille — reflecting our deep commitment to inclusion at every level of our work.

We believe Burundi's future depends on informed, confident, and empowered young people who have the tools and platforms to lead meaningful change.

Our Mission

To empower young Burundians with the skills, confidence, and platforms necessary to participate meaningfully in democratic processes and the 21st century economy.

Our Vision

A Burundi where every young person can advocate for themselves, engage respectfully with others, and contribute actively to sustainable national development.

Online Show

Babikora Gute?

"Babikora Gute?" — meaning "How do they do it?" in Kirundi — is our inclusion-focused online show that spotlights the lived experiences of people with disabilities in Burundi. Every episode gives a voice to individuals who navigate daily life, work, and community with resilience and dignity.

The show challenges stigma, breaks down barriers, and builds bridges — showing society that people with disabilities are not problems to be solved, but partners to be embraced. By sharing their stories, we invite communities to listen, learn, and work together toward a truly inclusive Burundi.

Watch our episodes live and on-demand on Facebook and Instagram.

Championnat Interscolaire

Gitega's First Inter-School Debate Championship

In January 2026, A.R.I.S.E. Initiative organized the first-ever inter-school students debate championship in Gitega — bringing together four schools in a celebration of young voices and ideas.

Under the theme "La parole des jeunes au service du changement" (Youth voice in service of change), students from four schools competed across two rounds of high-energy debate.

🏫

Participating Schools: Lycée Gitega (ENG) · ECOSO · CND · Lycée d'Excellence Musinzira

📍

Semi-Final: Gitega Study Space — January 10, 2026 at 13h30

🏆

Final: Alliance Française Gitega — January 17, 2026 at 14h30

🎟️

Entry: Free — open to all youth of Gitega
